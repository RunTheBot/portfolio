### 1. [µController — Custom Unrailed Game Controller](https://github.com/RunTheBot/uController)

A dedicated, ultra-compact handheld controller engineered specifically for precision tile movement in *Unrailed*. Features a custom 2-layer PCB with USB-C, integrated LiPo boost converter for a stable 5V rail, and analog hall effect sensors for zero-drift tactile gaming.

### 3. [Friend Tracker — Custom Meshtastic GPS & Compass](https://github.com/RunTheBot/Tracker)

A handheld LoRa-based offline tracking unit with GPS and electronic compass, designed for off-grid location sharing. Powered by a Seeed Wio-E5 / ESP32-S3 with u-blox MAX-M10S-00B GNSS, QMC5883P magnetometer, custom KiCad arc transform layout, and a 3D-printed Onshape parametric enclosure.
